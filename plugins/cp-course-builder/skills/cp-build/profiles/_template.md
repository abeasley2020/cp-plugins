# College profile: <college or program>

Copy this file to `<college-slug>.md` and fill it in at Gate 0. A profile adds to the standard and never lowers it.

## Learners

- Who they are, what they already know, and what they do at work.
- Expertise level: novice, mixed or experienced. This sets how fast worked examples fade (precedence rule 3).

## Credit and time

- Learner hours per credit hour, if the college expects something other than 45.
- Lesson length, if different from 10 to 15 minutes.

## Field language

- The words practitioners use, and the words to avoid.
- Units, notation and naming conventions.

## Real cases

- Where cases come from (faculty files only). Never invent one.

## Regulation and safety

- Rules that change often and must carry a source and a date (R5).
- Anything that needs a caution block.

## Assessment

- What an authentic task looks like in this field (R6).
- Graded items that live in Brightspace, outside the package.
