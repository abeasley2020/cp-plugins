# College profile: Agriculture and Food Science

## Learners

- Working professionals in food manufacturing, quality, regulatory, agronomy and extension roles, plus graduate students preparing for them.
- Expertise level: mixed. Assume field experience but not the science behind it. Start with complete worked examples; fade them after the course midpoint (M9, precedence rule 3).

## Credit and time

- 45 learner hours per credit hour unless the blueprint records otherwise in `decisions.md`.
- Lessons of 10 to 15 minutes.

## Field language

- Use the terms a plant or field team uses on the job: line, lot, hold, release, deviation, corrective action, verification, label claim.
- SI units with the customary unit in parentheses only where the industry still uses it.
- Spell out an acronym at first use in every lesson (M6), even when the last lesson defined it.

## Real cases

- Cases come from faculty slides, lectures, recalls the faculty cite, and audit or inspection findings the faculty supply. If the source has none, the problem block carries a `[GAP:]`.

## Regulation and safety

- Food safety, pesticide and labeling rules change. Every regulatory claim carries the faculty source and the date it was current (R5). Put the claim in a `:::caution` block when getting it wrong is a safety or legal risk.
- Never paraphrase a label, a regulation or a tolerance into a number the source does not give.

## Assessment

- Authentic tasks look like the work: an audit of a line, a corrective action plan, a label review, a hazard analysis for one product, a recommendation memo to a plant manager (R6).
- Graded quizzes and submissions live in Brightspace, outside the package. Lessons carry ungraded practice only.
