# Week 3 lecture notes: workers, harvest, and packing

## Worker health and hygiene
- Sick workers are the main source of norovirus and hepatitis A on produce.
- Train every worker, including family and volunteers, before they touch produce.
- Workers report vomiting, diarrhea, or jaundice and stay out of produce handling.

## Handwashing stations
- Soap, clean water, single-use towels, and a catch basin for wastewater.
- Within a quarter mile or a five minute walk of the work area.
- Hand sanitizer does not replace washing hands that have soil on them.

## Harvest
- Clean harvest containers, never used for trash or chemicals.
- Do not place containers directly on soil.
- Cull damaged produce; damage lets pathogens in.

## Packing area
- Clean, then sanitize food contact surfaces. Cleaning removes soil; sanitizing reduces microbes.
- Keep the packing area free of animals and standing water.
