# The pathogens that matter on a farm

Purpose: Recognize the bacteria and viruses most often linked to produce outbreaks and know where each one comes from, so you can tell which practices protect against which hazard.

## Two kinds of hazards, two kinds of sources

The microbes that cause produce outbreaks fall into two groups. **Bacteria** mostly come from animal feces and the environment. **Viruses** mostly come from people, especially workers who handle produce while sick.

Table: Pathogens linked to fresh produce and where they come from

| Pathogen | Type | Usual source on a farm |
|---|---|---|
| E. coli O157:H7 | Bacterium | Feces from cattle and other animals, carried by water, manure, or wildlife |
| Salmonella | Bacterium | Feces from animals including birds and reptiles; contaminated water |
| Listeria monocytogenes | Bacterium | Soil and damp packing areas; it can grow in cool, wet places |
| Norovirus | Virus | Sick workers, through hands and poor handwashing |
| Hepatitis A | Virus | Infected workers, sometimes before they feel ill |

> **Key idea**
>
> Match the control to the source. Water and manure practices target bacteria. Worker health and handwashing practices target viruses.

> **Flashcards: Retrieval practice**
>
> **Q:** Which pathogen can grow in a cool, damp packing shed?
>
> **A:** Listeria monocytogenes. It survives and grows in cool, wet environments, which is why packing area sanitation matters.
>
> **Q:** Where do norovirus and hepatitis A usually come from on a farm?
>
> **A:** People. Sick or infected workers transfer them through their hands.

> **Knowledge check**
>
> 1. A worker had vomiting last night and feels better this morning. Which hazard is the main concern if they harvest lettuce today?
>    - [ ] E. coli O157:H7 from soil
>    - [x] Norovirus from the worker
>    - [ ] Listeria from the packing shed
>
>    Why: Norovirus spreads from people and stays infectious after symptoms end. The other two come from the environment, not from this worker.

> **Check yourself**
>
> Which of these sources is present on your farm right now: livestock, wildlife, a pond, a packing shed, or seasonal workers?
