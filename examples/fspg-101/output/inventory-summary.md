# Inventory summary: FSPG 101 (sample)

## Course in one paragraph
A short online course for small farm owners, market garden managers, and extension volunteers on identifying food safety hazards on a produce farm and putting practical controls in place. Learners finish with a one-page farm food safety plan. The materials are written for a four-week live cohort.

## Outcomes and structure as the faculty wrote them
1. Explain how pathogens reach fresh produce and why produce eaten raw carries higher risk.
2. Assess agricultural water sources and choose an appropriate testing and treatment approach.
3. Apply safe handling practices for raw manure and compost on a small farm.
4. Design worker hygiene, harvest, and cleaning practices that reduce contamination.
5. Create a simple written farm food safety plan with records an inspector would accept.

Four weeks: (1) pathogens and routes, (2) water and soil amendments, (3) workers, harvest, packing, (4) cleaning, records, farm plan.
