Course map: Food Safety for Small-Scale Produce Growers

FSPG 101. College of Agriculture (sample course for testing). Draft for instructional designer and faculty review.

Course at a glance
ItemDetail
AudienceSmall farm owners, market garden managers, and extension volunteers; no science background assumed
DeliveryOnline, self-paced, on-demand (converted from a live cohort syllabus)
PackagingOne Brightspace package per module
Learner time232 minutes across 4 modules

Course outcomes

  1. CLO1 (Understand). Explain how pathogens reach fresh produce and why produce eaten raw carries higher risk.
  2. CLO2 (Evaluate). Assess agricultural water sources and choose an appropriate testing and treatment approach.
  3. CLO3 (Apply). Apply safe handling practices for raw manure and compost on a small farm.
  4. CLO4 (Create). Design worker hygiene, harvest, and cleaning practices that reduce contamination.
  5. CLO5 (Create). Create a written farm food safety plan with records an inspector would accept.
