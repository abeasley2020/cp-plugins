Purdue University Online FSPG 101 Module 01: Why produce safety matters

Activity: Walk your farm

Module 01, Activity 4 of 4. Estimated time: 15 minutes.

What this topic is for

Walk your own farm and record where each of the five routes shows up. Your notes become the first section of your farm food safety plan in Module 04.

What you will do

  1. Pick your example crop from topic 1.1.
  2. Walk the path that crop takes, from field to the point of sale.
  3. At each stop, write down which of the five routes could reach the crop there.
  4. Rate each risk as low, medium, or high, with one sentence of reasoning.
  5. Keep your notes. You will paste them into your farm plan in Module 04.

Compare your notes with a model answer

A strong set of notes names at least four stops, links each to a specific route, and gives a reason for each rating that points to something you saw. If you rated everything low, walk the farm again and look at water sources and animal signs first; they are the most often missed.

This activity is not graded

Your notes feed the graded farm plan in Module 04. The Module 01 quiz is in Brightspace Quizzes.
