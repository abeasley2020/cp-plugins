Module 01: Why produce safety matters overview

Module 01. Estimated time: 5 minutes.

In this module

Learners see why produce eaten raw carries risk, meet the pathogens that matter on a farm, and trace the five routes contamination travels.

Objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:

  1. Explain why fresh produce has no kill step between the field and the plate.
  2. Identify the bacteria and viruses most often linked to produce outbreaks and where each comes from.
  3. Trace the five routes of contamination on your own farm.
