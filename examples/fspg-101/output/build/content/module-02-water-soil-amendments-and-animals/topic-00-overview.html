Module 02: Water, soil amendments, and animals overview

Module 02. Estimated time: 5 minutes.

In this module

Learners rank water sources by risk, read a water test result, handle manure and compost safely, and decide what to do when animals get into a field.

Objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:

  1. Rank agricultural water sources by contamination risk and justify the ranking.
  2. Interpret a generic E. coli water test result and decide on a next step.
  3. Apply raw manure and compost handling practices, including application intervals and storage.
  4. Decide whether produce near animal intrusion can be harvested.
