Ranking your water sources

Module 02, Topic 1 of 5. Estimated time: 12 minutes.

What this topic is for

Rank the water sources on your farm by risk so you know where to focus testing and protection.

Not all water carries the same risk

Agricultural water sources ranked by contamination risk
SourceRelative riskWhy
Municipal or public waterLowestTreated and tested by the provider
Groundwater wellModerateProtected underground, but a damaged or uncapped wellhead lets contamination in
Surface water: ponds, streams, ditchesHighestOpen to animals, runoff, and upstream activity

How water reaches the crop

Risk also depends on how the water is applied. Drip irrigation that wets the soil carries less risk than overhead sprinklers that wet the edible part of the plant, especially close to harvest.

Retrieval practice

Commit to an answer before opening each card.

Which water source carries the highest risk, and why?

Surface water, because animals, runoff, and upstream activity can all reach it.

Why does drip irrigation carry less risk than overhead sprinklers?

Drip wets the soil, not the edible part of the plant.

Check yourself

List every water source you use, what you use it for, and how it touches the crop. You will need this list for the activity at the end of this module.
