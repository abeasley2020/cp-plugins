Purdue University Online FSPG 101 Module 02: Water, soil amendments, and animals

Activity: Assess your water source

Module 02, Activity 5 of 5. Estimated time: 20 minutes.

What this topic is for

Assess one water source on your farm and choose a testing and protection approach. This worksheet is graded.

What you will submit

A completed water source assessment worksheet for one source you use on a crop eaten raw. Submit it in the Brightspace assignment folder named Water source assessment worksheet. It is worth 25 points.

Steps

  1. Choose the source with the highest risk from your list in topic 2.1.
  2. Describe the source, what it is used for, and how it touches the crop.
  3. List what could contaminate it, using the five routes from Module 01.
  4. Record your most recent test result, or state that it has not been tested.
  5. Choose a next step: protect, test, treat, or change how the water is applied. Explain why.

A worked example

Sample worksheet entries for a farm pond
Worksheet fieldSample answer
SourceSpring-fed pond, about half an acre
UseOverhead irrigation on lettuce and spinach
Possible contaminationCattle on the neighboring pasture drink from the inlet; geese on the pond
Last testNot tested this season
Next stepSwitch the greens to drip from the well, test the pond monthly, and fence the inlet
How the worksheet is scored

Full credit goes to a worksheet that names a specific source, links each contamination risk to a route, uses a real or clearly missing test result, and gives a next step that matches the risk. The rubric in the assignment folder lists the points for each part.
