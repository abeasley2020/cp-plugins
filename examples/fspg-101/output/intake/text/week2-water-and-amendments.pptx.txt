Agricultural water sources
Municipal water: lowest risk
Groundwater wells: moderate
Surface water such as ponds and streams: highest

Testing your water
Generic E. coli as an indicator
Test before the season and during use
Keep lab reports
[notes] Explain geometric mean and statistical threshold in plain terms; do not get lost in the math.
[slide 3]
Raw manure and compost
Raw manure: apply well before harvest
Treated compost: documented time and temperature
Store piles away from fields and water
[notes] Discuss the 90 and 120 day intervals used by organic programs as an example.
[slide 4]
Wild and domestic animals
Look for signs of intrusion before harvest
Do not harvest within a buffer of fecal matter
Keep pets out of production areas
[notes] Farmers cannot fence out every animal; the goal is assessment and reasonable action.
