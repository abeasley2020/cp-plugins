Food Safety for Small-Scale Produce Growers
Course code: FSPG 101 (sample course for testing). Instructor: Dr. Jordan Rivera, Department of Food Science (fictional).
# Course description
This short online course prepares small farm owners, market garden managers, and extension volunteers to identify food safety hazards on a produce farm and put practical controls in place. It follows the risk areas used in produce safety training: workers, water, soil amendments, harvest and packing, and recordkeeping. Learners finish with a one-page farm food safety plan for their own operation.
# Learning outcomes
- Explain how pathogens reach fresh produce and why produce eaten raw carries higher risk.
- Assess agricultural water sources and choose an appropriate testing and treatment approach.
- Apply safe handling practices for raw manure and compost on a small farm.
- Design worker hygiene, harvest, and cleaning practices that reduce contamination.
- Create a simple written farm food safety plan with records an inspector would accept.
